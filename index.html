The Four Core Skills: OARS

This is how Partnership, Acceptance, Compassion, and Evocation actually get said out loud.

Can't be answered yes or no.

They invite your patient to talk, and to think out loud while they do it.

Genuine recognition of a strength or an effort.

Not empty praise. It has to be true, and it has to be specific.

Repeat or rephrase what your patient just said.

It proves you heard it. It also lets them hear it back, often for the first time.

Gather several threads of what's been said into one short recap.

Often ends in another open question, keeping the conversation moving.
