The Four Core Skills: OARS

This is how Partnership, Acceptance, Compassion, and Evocation actually get said out loud.

Select a core skill above to explore.

Can't be answered yes or no.

They invite your patient to talk, and to think out loud while they do it.

Clinical Example

"How has your breathing been affecting your day-to-day life recently?"

Genuine recognition of a strength or an effort.

Not empty praise. It has to be true, and it has to be specific.

Clinical Example

"It takes a lot of effort to even show up to these appointments when you're feeling this frustrated. I appreciate you being here."

Repeat or rephrase what your patient just said.

It proves you heard it. It also lets them hear it back, often for the first time.

Clinical Example

"You're tired of being lectured about your weight, but you also want to have enough energy to keep up with your granddaughter."

Gather several threads of what's been said into one short recap.

Often ends in another open question, keeping the conversation moving.

Clinical Example

"So, to summarize: you know the smoking is an issue, but right now it's your only stress relief at work, and you don't want to give that up until things settle down. Did I get that right?"
